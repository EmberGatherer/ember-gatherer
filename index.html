Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book catering?

For weddings and larger events, contact us as early as possible. Availability depends on the event date, guest count and menu.

Can I customize a catering package?

Yes. Share your preferred dishes and dietary requirements in the order form. Custom selections are confirmed by quote.

Are smoked meat prices per pound?

Yes. The published smoked meat prices are Canadian dollars per pound of finished product.

Do you offer delivery and buffet setup?

These services can be discussed when requesting a quote. Any additional fees will be confirmed before payment.

When is my event confirmed?

Submitting a request or completing online payment does not by itself guarantee event availability. Ember & Gatherer must confirm the event details.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary preferences?

Please list all allergies and dietary requirements in the order form. We will discuss what can be accommodated.
