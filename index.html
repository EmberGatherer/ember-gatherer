Frequently Asked Questions

Where do you provide catering? We are based in Edmonton, Alberta. Contact us to discuss your event location.
How much is delivery? Delivery is $15 CAD for orders under $100 CAD. Orders of $100 CAD or more qualify for free delivery.
Can I customize my catering menu? Yes. Contact us with your guest count, event date and menu preferences.
Do you cater weddings? Yes. We offer wedding buffet options, appetizer tables and custom catering arrangements.
How do I pay? Online checkout is designed to use secure Stripe payment processing.
Can I request special dietary options? Please include dietary requirements in the order notes so they can be reviewed.
